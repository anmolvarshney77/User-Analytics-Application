What just happened?

On load the tracker generated (or reused) a session UUID in localStorage and sent page_view. Each click you made sent click with the document-space coordinates of the click and the document's full width/height. The API stores them in MongoDB. The dashboard reads /api/sessions, /api/sessions/:id/events, and /api/heatmap?pageUrl=....

Tracker script: /tracker.js · API endpoint: /api/events
